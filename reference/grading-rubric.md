## 1. PER-COMPANY RUBRICS

### 1.1 PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator)

**Scale:** 1–10, half grades 1.5–8.5 (introduced 2008; exact launch date not re-verified). **There is no PSA 9.5.** [verified 2026-07] Grade is holistic ("gestalt") across centering, corners, edges, surface — no published subgrades. Same written standard for all eras and categories (sports and TCG). [PSA]

**Per-grade criteria table** (centering = official psacard.com/gradingstandards numbers, re-verified against agreeing mirrors 2026-07; descriptors = official language, condensed) [PSA]:

| Grade | Centering front | Centering back | Corners | Edges | Surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10 GEM-MT** | "approximately 55/45 to 60/40" [verified 2026-07] | 75/25 [verified 2026-07] | Four perfectly sharp | Clean | Sharp focus, full gloss, stain-free; ONE minor flaw allowed only if it doesn't hurt eye appeal (e.g., slight print imperfection) |
| **9 MINT** | 60/40 to 65/35 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 [verified 2026-07] | Sharp | Clean | ONE of: very slight wax stain on reverse, minor printing imperfection, slightly off-white borders |
| **8 NM-MT** | 65/35 to 70/30 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Slightest fraying on 1–2 corners | Clean | Minor printing imperfection; slightly off-white borders; "appears Mint 9 at first glance" |
| **7 NM** | 70/30 to 75/25 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Slight fraying on some corners | Minor | Slight surface wear on close inspection; minor print blemish; **wax staining OK on BACK only**; most gloss retained |
| **6 EX-MT** | 80/20 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Slightly graduated fraying | Very slight notching | Visible wear/print defect not detracting; very light scratch on close inspection; some gloss loss |
| **5 EX** | 85/15 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Very minor rounding becoming evident | Minor chipping | Gloss loss apparent; several light scratches; focus may be off-register |
| **4 VG-EX** | 85/15 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Slightly rounded | Noticeable | **First grade where a light crease is tolerated** [verified 2026-07] ; modest but noticeable wear; light scuffing |
| **3 VG** | 90/10 [verified 2026-07] | 90/10 | Some rounding | Noticeable wear | Crease may be visible; much gloss lost; borders may be yellowed; obverse staining possible |
| **2 GOOD** | 90/10 | 90/10 | Accelerated rounding | Chipping | **Several creases**; obvious wear/scratching/staining; gloss may be absent |
| **1.5 FR** | none stated | none stated | Extreme wear | — | Heavy creases, brown/dirty borders; **must be fully intact** |
| **1 PR** | none stated | none stated | — | — | May be missing one or two small pieces; major creasing; extreme discoloration |

- Ratio = larger border's share of total margin on that axis; **the worse axis governs**. Back centering is dramatically forgiven (75/25 still gems). [PSA]
- **Additional official leeway [verified 2026-07 — this is official PSA policy, not community lore]:** "A 5% leeway is given to the front centering minimum standards for cards which grade NM 7 or better." PSA's own example: a card meeting all other Mint 9 criteria at 60/40 automatically meets 9 centering; at 65/35 it *may* still meet 9 "if the eye appeal of the card is good." [PSA]
- **2024–25 change (critical):** PSA's published Gem 10 front language now reads "approximately 55/45 to 60/40" [verified 2026-07]; hobby press documented the quiet shift from a plain "60/40," with CEO Nat Turner quoted: "no change in day-to-day… that's how we've been operating for a very long time." [PSA — press-reported, not a PSA release] Treat true 10 centering as ~55/45, with 60/40 the outer edge only when everything else is flawless. Enforcement reportedly tightened in early 2024: GemRate data shows modern sports gem rates fell ~10–12 points mid-2024→mid-2025 (sports gem 43%→34%). [X-ERA — GemRate-reported]

**Qualifiers** (card meets ALL criteria for the numeric grade EXCEPT one attribute): OC off-center, MC miscut, ST stain, PD print defect, OF out of focus/register, MK marks ("any and all cards with writing, ink marks, pencil marks, or evidence of the impression left from the act of writing" — verbatim [verified 2026-07]). Six qualifiers total; two (MK, MC) are effectively mandatory when present [verified 2026-07]; submitters may request "no qualifiers," which lowers the numeric grade instead. **Market rule of thumb: a qualifier ≈ 2 grades of value** (PSA 6 OC trades near straight PSA 4) [community — verified as the prevailing market convention 2026-07, not PSA policy].

**No-Grade codes:** N1 trimming, N2 restoration, N3 recoloration, N4 questionable authenticity, N5 altered stock (includes crease pressing), N6 undersized, N7 cleaning/whitening, N8 severe miscut, N9 don't grade. Hand-cut issues gradeable only with visible borders on all four sides, else "Authentic." Since 2021, Genamint computer vision screens for trimming/alteration/crack-and-resub (~70 anomaly types — brief-sourced, not re-verified). [PSA]

**Eye appeal (official):** graders "reserve the right, based on the strength or weakness of the eye appeal, to make a judgment call" — bumps and knocks both happen. Tilt hurts eye appeal even inside measured tolerance. [PSA]

**Era adjustments — vintage forgiveness (label: policy vs practice):**
- **Documented official (qualitative only):** PSA's published numbers are era-agnostic. PSA's own editorial states print/registration must be judged "in context for the issue" ("it would be ludicrous to compare the registration on a 1948 Leaf Bob Feller to a 1966 Topps Sandy Koufax"). This is the *only* era-forgiveness statement traceable to PSA itself, and it is qualitative. [X-ERA — official PSA article]
- **Community-documented practice (NOT official PSA text — treat as folklore-grade evidence):** pre-1980 cards get forgiveness on surface (print lines, registration drift, edge roughness endemic to old presses) and centering at mid grades in practice. Note the written standard already allows 70/30–75/25 at NM 7 for *any* card; the community claim is that PSA applies these bands more charitably to vintage. A vintage mid-grade is defined mostly by **corners + creases**; a modern grade mostly by **centering + surface micro-flaws**. [community]
- **Set-specific factory norms (community/pop-report documented, not written policy):** O-Pee-Chee rough cuts documented at PSA 8+; 1971 Topps black-border chipping graded on merits but 7–8 is elite in pop context; 1952 Topps high-number "print snow" and diamond cuts still deduct via eye appeal; 1948–49 Leaf poor registration is normal for the issue. [community]
- **What era forgiveness does NOT extend to:** corners, creases, stains — graded on condition regardless of age. [X-ERA]
- **Numeric deduction anchors (derived from PSA's official per-grade text — era-agnostic, not vintage-specific):** single light crease ≈ cap 4; multiple/several creases ≈ 2; back wax stain tolerated at 7; front stain ≈ 3 or ST qualifier; paper loss ≤ 1.5; pen/pencil = MK. [PSA, verified 2026-07 against per-grade text]
- **Current-era caution:** 2024–25 crossover tests suggest today's PSA is the strictest of the majors on vintage — tighter than PSA's own 1990s–2000s output (old PSA slabs widely believed over-graded vs today). [X-ERA — small-sample tests + community consensus, see §6 caveats]
- Half grades skew vintage in practice; estimator should output them sparingly (4.5, 6.5, 8.5 on strong-for-grade vintage). [PSA — community-observed pattern]

**Base rates (priors — GemRate/press-reported, conflate submission quality with strictness):** 2025 H1 PSA overall gem rate 43% on 8.9M cards; **sports 34% / TCG 50%**; 2020s-era TCG ~59% gem. PSA graded ~20M items in 2025 per SI/GemRate recap [PSA] (the BGS brief's Collectors announcement figure is 18.3M cards — "items vs cards" definitions differ across sources; both are brief-sourced). Vintage inverts: 1952 Topps Mantle, ~1,656 graded — >97% grade 7 or lower (35 PSA 8, 6 PSA 9, 3 PSA 10; pop snapshot at brief date, drifts over time). **Prior: pack-fresh modern modal outcome is 9–10; handled vintage modal outcome is 2–5.** [PSA]

**Top-grade killers (modern/TCG) [community-documented, consistent across grader-adjacent sources]:** print lines (factory, still deducted), hairline holo scratches visible only in raking light ("half the unexpected 9s"), any visible edge/corner whitening (typically = 9 or below), one soft corner tip, centering just past 55/45. Scratches confined to non-focal areas may still 10; crossing the focal area caps at 9. [PSA]

### 1.2 BGS (Beckett Grading Services)

**Scale:** 1–10 in half points, with **four subgrades — Centering, Corners, Edges, Surface** — each in half points, all printed on the label (only major grader to do so). Authenticity/alteration check precedes grading; altered cards slabbed "Authentic – Altered." [BGS]

**Official per-grade table** (structure from beckett.com/grading/scale; top rows and 6/4 rows re-verified via agreeing mirrors 2026-07 — beckett.com blocks direct fetch; footnote: "These are not rules, only general guidelines") [BGS]:

| Grade | Centering front | Centering back | Corners | Edges | Surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pristine 10** | 50/50 all around [verified 2026-07] | mirrors split 55/45+ vs 60/40+ — treat 10 band as 55/45–60/40 back [verified 2026-07, discrepancy confirmed] | Perfect to naked eye, Mint under magnification | Perfect to naked eye, virtually flaw-free under mag | No print spots; flawless color/registration; perfect gloss, devoid of scratches and metallic print lines |
| **Gem Mint 9.5** | 50/50 one way, 55/45 other [verified 2026-07 via two mirrors; one mirror reads "55/45 both ways" — see §6 caveats] | 60/40+ [verified 2026-07] | Mint to naked eye; slight imperfections under mag | Virtually Mint; a speck of wear under intense scrutiny | A few extremely minor print spots under intense scrutiny only; perfect gloss; NO scratches or metallic print lines |
| **Mint 9** | 55/45 both ways [verified 2026-07] | 70/30+ [verified 2026-07] | Mint on close inspection; speck of wear allowed | Unobtrusive specks of chipping | Handful of print specks or one minor spot; 1–2 tiny scratches barely noticeable; ONE faint unobtrusive metallic print line |
| **NM-MT 8** | 60/40 both ways+ | 80/20+ | Sharp to naked eye, slight imperfections close-up | Relatively smooth; naked-eye chipping specks allowed | A few minor print spots; very minor color/focus flaws; very minor scratches close-up; subtle print line |
| **NM 7** | 65/35+ | 90/10+; very slight diamond cutting allowed | Very minor wear on 2–3 corners | Slight roughness, minor chipping | Noticeable print spots; minor border discoloration; very minor wax stain on back |
| **EX-MT 6** | 70/30 [verified 2026-07] | 95/5; slight diamond cut [brief-sourced back ratio, not re-verified] | Fuzzy but free of dings/fraying | Moderate roughness/chipping | Minor wax stains or extremely subtle ink; minor scratches, no scuffing |
| **EX 5** | 75/25 | 95/5 [brief-sourced] | Four fuzzy corners, touch of notching/minor ding | Noticeable roughness, no layering | Some gloss loss; minor scratches; very light ink |
| **VG-EX 4** | 80/20 [verified 2026-07] | 100/0 [verified 2026-07] | Slight notching/layering, moderate dings | Readily chipped/notched, slightly layered | **Hairline creases** (first crease grade) [verified 2026-07]; heavy print spots; light tape stain |
| **VG 3** | 85/15 | 100/0 | Slightly rounded/notched | Heavy notching, moderate layering | Very minor creases; very little gloss; very minor tear |
| **G 2** | 90/10 | 100/0 or offcut | Noticeably rounded, moderate layering | Severely chipped/layered | Noticeable creases; no gloss; noticeable tear |
| **P 1** | 100/0 or offcut | — | Heavily rounded/notched | Destructive damage | Heavy creases, severe stains, severe tear |

**Key hard threshold:** creases of any kind cap the Surface subgrade around 4 or below → final grade lands in the ≤4.5 band (weakest-link math below). A crease is the practical Mint-killer. [BGS]

**Final-grade algorithm** (official vs reverse-engineered, re-verified 2026-07):
1. NOT a simple average — "the lowest grade is the most heavily weighted." **[official Beckett language, verified 2026-07]**
2. Final is usually **lowest subgrade + 0.5**. **[community reverse-engineering — holds for "over 99%" of observed slabs, not official]**
3. **Tie rule:** if two or more subgrades tie at the lowest, final = that lowest value (9.5/8.5/8.5/9.5 → 8.5). **[community-verified, not official]**
4. Second-lowest subgrade caps the final. **[community]**
5. **Gem Mint 9.5 minimum:** at least three 9.5 subgrades, fourth no less than 9. **[official, verified 2026-07]**
6. **Pristine 10:** at least three 10 subs + fourth ≥9.5 (10/10/10/9 → 9.5) **[community-documented]**. **Black Label 10:** quad 10s only **[official, verified 2026-07]** (introduced Sept 2014 — brief-sourced date).
7. Outlier exception: final "rarely, if ever, exceeds two levels above the lowest" sub; official example 10/10/10/6 → **7**. **[official Beckett example, verified 2026-07]**

**Labels:** Black = quad-10 Pristine; Gold = Pristine 10 & Gem 9.5; Silver = everything else. **Black Label rarity: 20,946 issued all-time vs ~8.3M+ BGS items ≈ 0.25% of all submissions ever** (GemRate snapshot at brief date); TCG dominates the list; sells 3–15x standard 10s [market observation]. Benchmark: 1st Ed Base Charizard — ~125 PSA 10s exist, only 3 BGS Pristine 10s, **zero** Black Labels (pop snapshot, drifts). [BGS]

**Autograph scale (separate, does not lower card grade):** 10 bold/nearly perfect; 9 very light bubbling; 8 ≤10% hidden; 7 ≤20%; 6 ≤35%; 5 >50% hidden/missing. [BGS — brief-sourced, not re-verified]

**Era adjustments — BVG:**
- **BVG (Beckett Vintage Grading)**, cards 1981 and older; since 2005, **one overall grade, no subgrades** (stated reason: let a vintage card "stand on its overall positive aesthetic merits"). Era-appropriate tolerances applied to stock, centering, surface. [BGS]
- Market reality [community]: BVG is third-tier for vintage behind PSA and SGC; "many believe that BVG has long overgraded cards" — do NOT map BVG grades 1:1 to PSA value. Defunct BCCG grades run "probably 2–3 grades too high" [community]; treat BCCG slabs as raw-with-a-caveat. [BGS]
- Within BGS the printed standards are era-neutral but low bands explicitly forgive vintage artifacts: diamond cutting allowed from 7 down, back centering to 100/0 at 4 and below, wax stains named at 7/6. [BGS]

**Strictness profile:** strictest major at the top on **centering** (9.5 needs 50/50–55/45 front vs PSA 10's ~55/45–60/40) and on **surface** at the 9.5 line (bans ALL scratches and metallic print lines; one faint line only allowed at 9); graders work under magnification. Surface is the most common gem-killer on chrome/refractor/holo [community]. PSA is comparatively harsher on corners at the 10 line, more lenient on centering. [BGS]

**Crossover facts [community/market estimates — wide error bars]:** BGS 9.5 → PSA 10: in-slab **<10%**; cracked raw **~50–60%**; quad-9.5s 60–70%; only ~40% of BGS 9.5s earn PSA 10 per a separate estimate — i.e., **BGS 9.5 sits between PSA 9 and PSA 10**. Best crossover profile: weak sub = centering (PSA forgives); worst: weak sub = corners (PSA hammers). PSA 10s frequently come back BGS 9.5. [BGS, X-ERA]

**Base rates:** BGS all-time gem rate (9.5+) ≈ 38%; 2025 TCG gem rate 60% (highest of majors); 2025 volume ~790K cards. Collectors (PSA parent) signed to acquire Beckett Dec 15, 2025; Beckett continues as independent brand. [BGS — brief-sourced]

### 1.3 SGC (Sportscard Guaranty Corporation)

**Scale:** 1–10 with half grades at every level, plus two distinct 10s. 100-point scale retired in 2018. Acquired by Collectors (PSA parent) Feb 2024 [brief-sourced]; repositioned as boutique **vintage** brand — by fall 2025 over half of SGC volume was pre-2000 cards. [SGC-CGC]

**Official per-grade table** (gosgc.com/card-grading/scale via official-text mirror, re-verified 2026-07; centering tolerance applies **front AND back** per the official scale [verified 2026-07] — third-party cross-studies read some backs looser, see §2 and §6 caveats) [SGC-CGC]:

| Grade | Centering | Key criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **10 PRI (Pristine)** | 50/50 [verified 2026-07] | Four sharp corners; crisp focus; no gloss breaks, print/refractor lines, stains; "no visible wear under magnification" |
| **10 GM (Gem Mint)** | 55/45+ [verified 2026-07] | Same, but a "slight print spot under close scrutiny" allowed if not distracting |
| **9.5 (Mint+)** | 55/45+ [verified 2026-07] | Looks like a 10 GM at first glance; tiny flaws closer up |
| **9 (Mint)** | 60/40+ [verified 2026-07] | Four sharp corners; ONE minor flaw: slight corner nick, small gloss break, surface scratch, minor print/refractor line, small print spot, minor focus/color issue |
| **8.5 (NM/MT+)** | 65/35 [verified 2026-07] | Four sharp corners; a few minor flaws from same list |
| **8 (NM/MT)** | 65/35 | Corners sharp to naked eye, slight wear under magnification |
| **7.5 (NM+)** | 70/30 | Very minor wear on one corner + minor surface flaws |
| **7 (NM)** | 70/30 | Slight wear some corners; small scratches/print spots; skewed diamond cut OK |
| **6.5 / 6 (EX/NM)** | 75/25 [verified 2026-07] | Slight corner fuzzing; slight edge notching; skewed diamond cut may exist (more noticeable at 6) |
| **5.5 / 5 (EX)** | 80/20 [verified 2026-07] | Minor rounding/fuzziness; edge chipping or roughness (no layering); **ONE "ever so slight" surface or "spider" crease may exist on ONE side (allowed at both 5.5 and 5) — SGC's crease line** [verified 2026-07] |
| **4.5 / 4 (VG/EX)** | 85/15 [verified 2026-07] | Slightly rounded corners; light hairline crease acceptable (may show on one or both sides at 4) |
| **3.5 / 3 (VG)** | 90/10 [verified 2026-07] | More rounded corners (not excessive); stronger creasing; poorer focus/registration; discoloration |
| **2.5 / 2 (Good)** | 90/10 | Rounded/frayed corners; heavy creasing and/or print spots; focus and discoloration imperfections |
| **1.5 (Fair)** | 90/10 | Heavy creasing; several major flaws; small portion of card may be missing |
| **1 (Poor)** | n/a | Multiple severe flaws; portions missing |

**Structural note:** SGC's first-allowed-crease sits at **5.5/5** (one slight spider crease) — more forgiving than PSA (crease first tolerated at 4) and CGC (one light crease at 4.5). **SGC net-grades rather than using qualifiers** — the same off-center vintage card can be PSA 7(OC) or SGC ~5. [SGC-CGC, X-ERA — the net-grading practice is community/brief-documented, not scale text]

**Era adjustments — vintage:**
- SGC is the vintage benchmark [community consensus]: "vintage-trained graders… trusted for T206-era, 1950s-60s Topps"; largest share of 1950s–70s cards among all graders; PSA–SGC volume gap under 2x only in early vintage/prewar. Correct model: **not looser standards, but era-appropriate expectations + heavy eye-appeal weighting** — well-presented vintage isn't nuked for era-typical print/registration quirks. [SGC-CGC — community/market-documented, not written SGC policy]
- SGC-vs-PSA vintage centering: community split (some find SGC tougher, some more OC-tolerant) — treat as a coin flip, ±0.5–1 grade. [X-ERA — community]
- Market: pre-1970 SGC slabs sell neck-and-neck with PSA (tuxedo-holder effect), sometimes better; **modern SGC 10 sells 20–40% under PSA 10**; the SGC-vintage premium has softened post-acquisition (1953 Topps Mays SGC 8: ~$48.8K late 2025 vs $186K PSA 8 comp). [SGC-CGC — market snapshots]

**Base rates:** 2025 overall gem rate **21% — lowest of the majors**, attributed to vintage-heavy submission mix, not necessarily strictness. Minor TCG player ("best for vintage Pokemon or bulk savings" [community]). [SGC-CGC]

### 1.4 CGC Cards

**Scale:** 1–10 with half grades, three-tier top. CGC Trading Cards launched 2020, CSG sports 2021, merged as "CGC Cards" mid-2023 (announced June 2023, unified scale live from mid-July 2023) [verified 2026-07]; second-largest card grader; 2025: 4.92M cards, ~25% of TCG market [brief-sourced]. [SGC-CGC]

**Official per-grade table** (cgccards.com/card-grading/grading-scale, re-verified direct 2026-07) [SGC-CGC]:

| Grade | Centering front / back | Key criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Pristine 10** | 50/50 | Virtually flawless to naked eye; flawless color/registration; **flawless under 10x magnification**; special label [verified 2026-07] |
| **Gem Mint 10** | ≤55/45 / ≤75/25 [verified 2026-07] | Overall 10 where ONE criterion misses Pristine; corners perfect to naked eye, Mint+ under 10x; no print spots; perfect gloss |
| **Mint+ 9.5** | — | "Premium eye appeal for a Mint card" [verified 2026-07] |
| **Mint 9** | 60/40 / 90/10 [verified 2026-07] | Four sharp corners, minor wear only; all original gloss; a few specks or ONE minor spot/surface defect |
| **NM/Mint 8.5–8** | 65/35 (at 8) [verified 2026-07] | Smooth edges, minor touches of wear; corners sharp to naked eye, slight imperfections under magnification |
| **NM 7.5–7** | 65/35 (7.5) – 70/30 (7) [verified 2026-07] | Touch of wear on 2–3 corners (7.5) / 3+ (7); very slight wax staining allowed on reverse |
| **EX/NM 6.5–6** | 75/25 (at 6) [verified 2026-07] | One slightly dinged corner OR two of: fuzzy corners, rough edges, noticeable print spots; **light wax staining on FRONT acceptable at 6.5 and 6** [verified 2026-07 — corrected: front-stain allowance starts at 6.5, not 6] |
| **EX 5.5–5** | — | Fuzziness/very minor rounding; one or two dinged corners (5.5) / two to three (5); edge chipping |
| **VG/EX 4.5–4** | 85/15 [verified 2026-07] | **One light crease (4.5) / one or more light creases (4) — CGC's crease line** [verified 2026-07]; scuffing, scratches; minor staining |
| **VG 3.5–3** | 90/10 [verified 2026-07] | Four rounded corners; one moderate crease or more than one light crease; gloss loss |
| **Good 2.5–2** | — | Heavier creasing — not edge-to-edge at 2.5, may run edge-to-edge at 2; **writing first allowed at 2.5 (one small mark, back only); small amount front or back at 2** [verified 2026-07 — corrected from "writing allowed at 2"] |
| **Fair 1.5** | — | One catastrophic flaw (staple hole, small area of missing surface, severe creasing and/or writing) |
| **Poor 1** | — | Major surface damage (severe creasing breaking surface); may be missing a small portion of cardstock |

Designations: Authentic (AU), Authentic Altered (AA — trimmed/recolored), Authentic ART. Separate autograph scale 5–10, no half grades. [verified 2026-07]

**Scale history (critical for reading slabs/pops):** pre-mid-2023 the top was Perfect 10 > Pristine 10 > Gem Mint 9.5, with optional subgrades. **Mid-2023 (announced June, effective from mid-July): subgrades eliminated; all prior Gem Mint 9.5s automatically re-designated Gem Mint 10 (updated online; physical label requires reholder); Perfect 10 retired — old Perfect 10 and old Pristine 10 both map to the new Pristine 10.** [verified 2026-07] An old-label CGC 9.5 is officially a today-10; legacy Perfect 10 / pre-2023 Pristine labels carry big premiums [market observation]. Any pre-mid-2023 CGC data uses the old scale. [SGC-CGC, X-ERA]

**Category position:** old-scale CGC was famously strict, especially on centering ("a card that might squeak by as a PSA 10 could get a CGC 9 or 9.5") [community]. Post-rescale numbers look mainstream — **2025 TCG gem rate 55%** [brief-sourced] — yet collector experience still runs strict at the very top ("most of my PSA 10s can't get CGC 9.5 [old-scale]") [community]; post-2023 consensus: **CGC is the strictest top-grade TCG grader** [community consensus]; strongest at Japanese-card consistency [community]. CGC 10s crossed to PSA often gem (20–40% crossover upside) [community estimate]. [SGC-CGC, X-ERA]

**Market mapping:** CGC Gem Mint 10 sells 10–30% below PSA 10; **CGC Pristine 10 commands ~1.2–2x a PSA 10**. [SGC-CGC — market snapshots]

**Era note:** CGC has no separate vintage division; its published low-grade bands carry the vintage forgiveness (diamond cut named in scale text at NM band per brief; "extreme diamond cut" allowance at 4 is brief-sourced and was NOT found in the current scale text — treat as unconfirmed; front wax stain at 6.5/6 is confirmed). Treat CGC's vintage-sports strictness as between PSA (currently strictest) and BVG (lenient) **[inference — the National 2025 test showed CGC above PSA on the same vintage cards, but sample was 3 cards]**. [X-ERA]

### 1.5 TAG and other graders (brief)

**TAG (Technical Authentication & Grading)** — computer-vision grading (photometric stereoscopic imaging), no human graders. **1000-point TAG Score: Pristine 10 = 990–1000, Gem Mint 10 = 950–989, Mint 9 = 900–949** [verified 2026-07 direct from taggrading.com], then ~50-point bands down (8.5 = 850–899, 8 = 800–849 [verified 2026-07]; Poor 1 = 100–149 brief-sourced). **No 9.5 — half points exist "on every grade level except for between 9 and 10"** [verified 2026-07, official language]. Centering: TAG scores per-attribute algorithmically and tolerances shift per card; third-party readings put the top-tier front bar around 51/49–53/47 with a much looser back (~70/30) **[third-party approximations — TAG's own scale page does not publish flat ratios, and third-party tables conflict with each other; treat as indicative only]**. The claim of different back specs for sports vs TCG is **[unsupported — killed; one third-party source explicitly reads the specs as identical across card types]**. Every slab gets a DIG report (defect maps, per-attribute scores, centering to a decimal, 800x zoom, surface-defect transparency slider). Scope: standard-size, **1989-present**, ≤50pt stock only [brief-sourced]. Monthly volume passed SGC by late 2025 [brief-sourced]. TAG's rubric + DIG taxonomy is the best public template for a vision estimator (feature claims, not accuracy studies). [SGC-CGC, PHOTO]

**HGA** — hybrid grading, documented credibility failures (fake Brady auto authenticated, counterfeit rookies slabbed, label math errors, glued slabs, BBB complaints, website shutdown). Treat HGA grades as weak signals; resale heavily discounted. [SGC-CGC]

**ISA** — ISA 10 requires 50/50–55/45 front (stricter on paper than PSA), but the market applies an adverse-selection discount to all minor slabs; informational only, low market weight. [SGC-CGC — brief-sourced]

**AGS (robograding, context for estimator design)** — 3D laser scanning; 8 subgrades (C/S/E/C × front/back); final = (front avg × 0.6) + (back avg × 0.4), rounded to nearest 0.5, capped at **lowest subgrade + 1**. [PHOTO — vendor-published method]

**Ximilar (API template)** — per-corner and per-edge grades; centering as L/R + T/B ratios with pixel margins; final = geomean(geomean(corners), geomean(edges), surface, centering); **70% front / 30% back**; positions itself as "best suited for soft grading or pre-grading." [PHOTO — vendor-published method]

## 3. THE PHOTO-ASSESSABILITY CONTRACT

What a vision model may estimate vs must hedge, from 1–2 flat photos. [PHOTO, PSA, BGS, SGC-CGC, X-ERA]

### 3.1 MAY estimate (objective geometry / naked-eye features — always with stated measurement uncertainty)

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Front centering ratios** | The single most trustworthy photo measurement, but still carries **±3–5 point error** from border-edge ambiguity and perspective — report approximate L/R and T/B numbers WITH an error band, never single-percent precision; company-differentiating (it alone separates 50/50 Pristine tiers from 55/45 Gem tiers) |
| **Back centering** | Equally computable — ONLY if back photo provided; gates PSA 10 (75/25), BGS 9.5 (60/40), CGC 9 (90/10) |
| Corner sharpness/whitening/rounding at zoom | Per-corner; naked-eye level only |
| Edge whitening/chipping on dark borders | High signal (Pokemon blue backs, black-bordered sets: "even one visible white dot often drops a 10 to a 9" — [community heuristic, strong signal but not a written rule]) |
| Gross surface damage | Visible creases, stains, tears, paper loss, large scratches, major print defects, writing |
| Miscut / rough cut / tilt / diamond cut | Directly visible |
| Focus / print registration | Medium reliability |

### 3.2 MUST hedge (state "not assessable from these photos" — never score clean)

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Hairline & holo surface scratches | Need raking/oblique light, often at one rotation only; cause of "half the unexpected 9s" [community]; small CV models "hallucinate scratches that aren't there — and miss ones that are" |
| Print lines / roller marks | Need angled light/magnification |
| Subtle creases, dents, indentations | Depth defects — the reason AGS built lasers and TAG built photometric stereo; "a clean card in scans is most likely an indent"; an undetectable light crease caps ~PSA 4 band (official crease standards) and is **the single largest downside risk in any photo estimate** |
| Warping / foil curl | Needs edge-on view or depth data; MTG curl cap ~8 is community folklore but the direction is right |
| Gloss | Needs reflection cues; weak evidence either way |
| Sub-millimeter corner touches | Graders use ~10x magnification; SGC Pristine/CGC Pristine/BGS 10 are magnification-gated — **never assert a 10-tier subcomponent from photos** |
| Anything through sleeve/toploader/slab | Plastic hotspots hide defects and create false ones |
| Trimming / alteration / authenticity | Out of scope entirely; pros disagree in-hand |
| Entire back, if no back photo | 30–40% of grade weight unverifiable (AGS weights back 40%, Ximilar 30% — vendor methods, cited as design precedent) |

### 3.3 Confidence & range phrasing rules

1. **Ranges, not points.** Output per-company grade RANGES with a most-likely value. Precedent: PSA's own Photograde frames photo comparison as legitimate estimation, "not a substitute for certification." [PHOTO]
2. **Asymmetric ranges.** Ceiling = what the photo supports; floor = hidden-defect risk (invisible crease/hairline). E.g., "photo-supported ceiling PSA 9; estimated range 7–9." [PHOTO]
3. **Modern glossy/holo:** assume ~0.5–1 grade downside risk on surface; **never assert 10 vs 9 confidently from a single flat image**. [PSA]
4. **No back photo:** cap the ceiling (a 10/Gem estimate is unavailable — back centering and back edges unverified) and widen the range. [PHOTO]
5. **Confidence tiers:** high / medium / low, tied to photo quality + coverage; high only with both sides, sharp, glare-free, raw card. When the model cannot support at least LOW with a defensible range, **decline and request retakes** rather than guess (industry precedent: SnapGradeAI refunds estimates below its internal 70%-confidence floor — **[vendor policy, cited as precedent only]**). [PHOTO]
6. **Image-quality gate first:** card ≥~80% of frame, in focus, all four borders visible, no sleeve/slab, no glare hotspot, not edited/AI-upscaled (fake-sharp "cartoon" corners), roughly perpendicular (perspective distortion silently corrupts centering ratios). One vendor claims its optimal protocol yields estimates within 0.5 points 94% of the time vs 72% with flash/filters/angles — **[vendor marketing claim, unverified; keep the protocol, not the numbers]**. [PHOTO]
7. **Never assert trim/alteration** — flag "unusually sharp edges / suspicious border ratios, professional review needed" at most. [X-ERA]
8. **Slabbed/sleeved input:** comment only on what's visible; never grade surface with high confidence through plastic. [PHOTO]
9. **Aggregation:** subscores per attribute → weakest-link logic (BGS official weighting language; AGS cap = lowest sub + 1 mirrors how PSA/BGS behave). [PHOTO, BGS]
10. **Universal disclaimer** on every estimate: pre-grade estimate, not a guarantee; graders inspect under magnification and raking light and routinely find defects photos cannot show. [PHOTO]
11. **Optional power feature:** accept a third tilted/raking-light photo to unlock surface confidence — the cheapest way to close the biggest blind spot. [PHOTO]
12. **Measurement honesty (added in verification pass):** centering estimates carry ±3–5 point error from a photo; when a measured ratio falls within that error band of a company's grade gate, the output range MUST straddle both grades — never let a borderline measurement silently pick a side.

## 4. VISION SYSTEM PROMPT (ready to use, verbatim)

```
You are the CardOps Grade Estimator, a vision pre-grader for trading cards. You receive 1-3 photos of a single card (front required; back and angled/raking-light shots optional) plus metadata: category (vintage_sports | modern_sports | tcg), era/year if known, set/language if known. You output ESTIMATED GRADE RANGES for PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC (and TAG when the card is 1989+ standard size), with rationale and confidence. You are a pre-grading tool, never a guarantee.

== STEP 0: IMAGE QUALITY GATE ==
Before estimating, check: card fills most of the frame; in focus; all four borders fully visible; no sleeve, toploader, or slab; no major glare hotspot; image not obviously filtered or AI-upscaled (beware fake-sharp corners); shot roughly perpendicular (perspective skew corrupts centering math).
- If the gate fails: DECLINE to estimate. State exactly what to reshoot and why. Do not guess.
- If the card is in a slab or sleeve: describe only what is visible, state that plastic prevents surface assessment, and mark overall confidence LOW.

== STEP 1: MEASURE (photo-verifiable facts, with honest error bars) ==
1. FRONT CENTERING: estimate border ratios on both axes as approximate numbers WITH uncertainty (e.g., "~58/42 L-R (+/-4), ~55/45 T-B (+/-4)"). Photo centering carries +/-3-5 points of error from border ambiguity and perspective - never imply single-point precision. The worse axis governs. If a measurement is within its error band of a company's grade gate, your range MUST straddle both grades. Note any tilt/diamond cut.
2. BACK CENTERING: same, only if a back photo exists.
3. CORNERS: assess each of the 4 corners (per side if back provided) for whitening, rounding, fraying, dings - naked-eye level only.
4. EDGES: whitening/chipping/notching; weight dark borders heavily (community heuristic: one clearly visible white dot on a dark border usually means 9 or below - treat as strong signal, not a hard rule).
5. SURFACE (visible only): creases, stains, tears, paper loss, large scratches, print defects, writing, focus/registration.
6. RED FLAGS: miscut/adjacent card visible, writing (PSA MK), suspicious border/edge geometry (flag "professional review for possible trim" - NEVER assert trimming or alteration).

== STEP 2: WHAT YOU MUST HEDGE ==
Always list as "not assessable from these photos" unless an angled/raking-light photo was provided: hairline scratches, holo/foil scratches, print lines, subtle creases and indentations, warping/foil curl, gloss condition, back condition (if no back photo), sub-millimeter corner wear (all companies' top tiers are magnification-gated). A clean-looking flat photo is WEAK evidence of a clean surface. Never assert 10-vs-9 from a single flat image.

== STEP 3: KEY NUMBERS (embed in reasoning; all from official published standards unless noted) ==
FRONT CENTERING GATES (worse axis, front / back):
- PSA: 10 = approx 55/45 to 60/40 / 75/25 (2024+ enforcement reportedly treats ~55/45 as the realistic bar); 9 = 60/40-65/35 / 90/10; 8 = 65/35-70/30 / 90/10; 7 = 70/30-75/25 / 90/10; 6 = 80/20; 5 = 85/15; 4 = 85/15; 3-2 = 90/10. Official PSA policy: a 5% front-centering leeway applies at NM 7 and above when eye appeal is good. No PSA 9.5 exists; half grades only 1.5-8.5, use sparingly.
- BGS: 9.5 = 50/50 one way 55/45 other / 60/40 back; 9 = 55/45 / 70/30; 8 = 60/40 / 80/20; 7 = 65/35 / 90/10; 6 = 70/30; 5 = 75/25; 4 = 80/20. Pristine 10 = 50/50 front (back band 55/45-60/40; sources split); Black Label = all four subgrades 10.
- SGC: Pristine 10 = 50/50; Gem 10 = 55/45; 9.5 = 55/45; 9 = 60/40; 8.5-8 = 65/35; 7.5-7 = 70/30; 6.5-6 = 75/25; 5.5-5 = 80/20; 4.5-4 = 85/15; 3.5 and below = 90/10 (SGC tolerances apply to front AND back).
- CGC: Pristine 10 = 50/50 (flawless under 10x - unverifiable from photos); Gem 10 = 55/45 / 75/25; 9 = 60/40 / 90/10; 8 = 65/35; 7.5 = 65/35; 7 = 70/30; 6 = 75/25; 4.5-4 = 85/15; 3.5-3 = 90/10.
- TAG (1989+ only): no 9.5 tier exists (990-1000 = Pristine, 950-989 = Gem Mint 10, 900-949 = Mint 9); TAG's centering tolerances are algorithmic and shift per card - estimate against the PSA/CGC gates and present TAG as a score band, not a ratio claim.
CREASE RULES (hard caps, from official per-grade standards): any visible crease -> PSA cap ~4 (light, single; several creases -> 2); BGS surface sub <=4 -> final lands in the <=4.5 band (hairline creases first named at BGS 4); SGC allows ONE slight "spider" crease at 5.5-5, hairline at 4.5-4; CGC one light crease = 4.5, one or more = 4, moderate/multiple = 3.5-3.
BGS SUBGRADE MATH: estimate four subs (centering/corners/edges/surface). Official: the lowest sub is the most heavily weighted; 9.5 requires three 9.5s and nothing below 9; final rarely if ever exceeds two levels above the lowest sub (official example: 10/10/10/6 -> 7). Community-derived (reliable in practice, not official): final = lowest sub + 0.5; two subs tied at lowest -> final = that value; Pristine 10 = three 10s + a 9.5.
OTHER HARD RULES: front stain -> PSA ~3 or ST; back wax stain tolerated at PSA/BGS/CGC 7; writing -> PSA MK, CGC 2.5-2 territory (2.5 = one small mark on back, 2 = small amount front or back); paper loss -> 1.5 or below; MTG foil curl visible -> cap ~8 (community-documented rule of thumb, not written standard).

== STEP 4: ERA & CATEGORY ADJUSTMENTS ==
- VINTAGE SPORTS (pre-1980): PSA's official (qualitative) guidance is to judge print quality/registration/rough cuts "in context for the issue" - shift the print/registration/stock assessment ~1 grade lenient (community-documented practice: O-Pee-Chee rough cuts can be 8+; 1971 Topps black-border chipping, 1952 high-number print snow, 1948-49 Leaf registration are era-normal). Corners, creases, and stains get NO era forgiveness. Expect centering to be the ceiling (70/30+ is common -> caps at 7 or PSA OC territory). Grade priors: handled vintage modal outcome is 2-5; treat 8+ estimates as exceptional claims requiring exceptional photo evidence. Community consensus from small-sample crossover tests: current PSA grades vintage strictest of the majors; BGS/BVG most lenient (single grade, no subgrades, market-discounted).
- MODERN SPORTS: centering + surface micro-flaws decide everything; PSA sports gem rate ~34% (2025, GemRate). PSA tightened centering enforcement in 2024 - do not give 60/40 cards a "likely 10."
- TCG/POKEMON: effectively NO era forgiveness (community-documented, pop-report-supported - no company publishes a TCG era policy) - WOTC-era is held to the same 55/45 standard (Base Set era gem rates ~1-3%, pop-derived prior). English prints run more off-center than Japanese (Japanese gem roughly 2-3x more often, pop-derived). The back photo matters extra: dark/blue backs reveal offset and edge whitening (silvering) easily. Holo surface is the #1 gem-killer and is usually NOT visible in flat photos - keep TCG holo surface uncertainty wide. TCG corners are die-cut rounded - judge wear, not shape.
- COMPANY PERSONALITIES (for range positioning; blend of official standards + community crossover experience): PSA = centering-lenient at the top, harsh on corners and surface, qualifier system (OC/MK/ST/PD/OF/MC; market treats a qualifier as ~2 grades lower); BGS = strictest centering and surface at 9.5+ on paper, weakest-link math; CGC = strictest top-grade for TCG per post-2023 consensus, Pristine tier above Gem; SGC = net-grades (no qualifiers - off-centering lowers the number), vintage-calibrated, crease-forgiving to 5.5-5. Rough equivalences (community estimates): BGS 9.5 sits between PSA 9 and PSA 10; CGC Gem 10 ~ PSA 10 minus a notch; SGC 10 ~ PSA 10 condition-wise.

== STEP 5: OUTPUT ==
Return JSON:
{
  "image_quality": {"pass": bool, "issues": [...], "back_photo": bool, "raking_photo": bool},
  "measurements": {
    "centering_front": {"lr": "~58/42 (+/-4)", "tb": "~55/45 (+/-4)"},
    "centering_back": {"lr": "...", "tb": "..."} | null,
    "corners": {"front_tl": "...", "front_tr": "...", "front_bl": "...", "front_br": "...", "back": "..." | null},
    "edges": "...", "surface_visible": "...", "red_flags": [...]
  },
  "estimates": {
    "PSA":  {"range": "7-9", "most_likely": "8", "ceiling_note": "photo-supported ceiling 9; no PSA 9.5 exists", "rationale": "..."},
    "BGS":  {"range": "...", "most_likely": "...", "subgrade_estimates": {"centering": "9-9.5", "corners": "...", "edges": "...", "surface": "8.5-9.5 (widest band - not photo-verifiable)"}, "rationale": "..."},
    "SGC":  {"range": "...", "most_likely": "...", "rationale": "..."},
    "CGC":  {"range": "...", "most_likely": "...", "pristine_possible": false, "rationale": "..."},
    "TAG":  {"range": "...", "most_likely": "...", "rationale": "..."} | null
  },
  "confidence": {"tier": "high|medium|low", "drivers": ["back photo present", "no raking-light shot - surface unverified", ...]},
  "not_assessable": ["surface hairlines/print lines", "back centering (no back photo)", ...],
  "disclaimer": "Pre-grade estimate, not a guarantee. Professional graders inspect under magnification and raking light and routinely find defects photos cannot show."
}
RANGE RULES: ranges are ASYMMETRIC - the ceiling is what the photos support, the floor prices in hidden-defect risk (an invisible crease or hairline is the largest downside). Confidence is a TIER plus drivers - never output a numeric confidence percentage; a photo model cannot calibrate one. Widen every range by at least one grade step when: no back photo (also cap ceiling below gem), holo/foil card without a raking-light shot, any glare over the card face, or any centering measurement within its error band of a grade gate. Confidence HIGH requires: both sides, sharp, glare-free, raw card, non-holo or raking shot provided. If even a LOW-confidence defensible range is not possible, output no estimate and request specific retakes. Report half grades sparingly and never output PSA 9.5. Never state a card IS a grade - only "estimated range." Never assert trimming, alteration, or authenticity.
```
